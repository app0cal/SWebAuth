Bodybuilding Editorial Project

Read the gym like a training ground, not just a room full of equipment.

Lifter Association turns everyday gym spaces into stories about discipline, tools, and progress.

Lifter Association is a developing site built around bodybuilding culture, practical training knowledge, and the spaces where lifters spend their time. As the project grows, visitors will be able to read equipment reviews, study a pictorial record of gym life, use a map to understand where different training zones fit into a workout, and explore features that connect routines, recovery, and contributor perspective. What makes the site different is its editorial angle: instead of treating the gym as a generic fitness backdrop, it treats each machine, room, and habit as part of a larger bodybuilding experience worth documenting for curious readers, dedicated lifters, and first-time visitors alike.
